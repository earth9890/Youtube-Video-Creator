## Description
91% of the things anxious people worried about never happened — and a third of the rest turned out better than expected.
In 90 seconds: why your mind replays the worst case, and 3 research-backed steps to break the loop.

Overthinking feels like control, but replaying the same scene a hundred times changes nothing.
This video walks through three small moves: write the worry down, give it a 15-minute appointment, and ask "can I act on this today?"
Your thoughts are not facts. They're weather. They pass.
