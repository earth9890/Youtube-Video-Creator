# Episode 40: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **What If You Drank 100 Energy Drinks?** (36 chars): the title question, said aloud at ~0:20 and answered in the last chapter. It's Zenn's "What If…?" shape, and the kid-like absurd number is the reason to click.
2. What Happens If You Drink 100 Energy Drinks? (44): the same hook, more searchable, flatter.
3. Why Energy Drinks Don't Give You Energy (39): true (no calories in caffeine; it hides tiredness), but it's the chapter-3 turn, not the whole story.
4. What Energy Drinks Actually Do to Your Brain (44): "Actually" signals the myth-bust; it gives up the escalation and the twist.
5. Caffeine Doesn't Give You Energy (32): short and true; good for a Short cut from "The chairs" chapter.

Not used: "The Most Dangerous Ingredient in Energy Drinks". The water twist is true at a hundred cans, but as a general claim it would mislead people, since caffeine is the real everyday risk.
