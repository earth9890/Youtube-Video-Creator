# Episode 78 "What If You Could Regrow Any Body Part?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Poppy, Mister Pike, Gerald, Mrs Okafor, the bagel and the Tooth Fairy are story devices. The rule card ("you regrow like a salamander does") is the story's premise; nothing here says an adult human can regrow a tooth. Everything the narrator, Doc, Brain, Poppy or Mister Pike states as fact is below. **Solid** = published source. **Inference** = our arithmetic. Quotes are exact as returned by the page.

## Checked and NOT used
- **Newt eye-lens regeneration**: newts regrow a lens throughout life; axolotls only in the first two weeks after hatching (PMC6679358). Cut for length.
- **Axolotl scar-free healing** ("starts by scar-free wound healing"): in the source, but the script has no space to explain the human scar; cut.
- **The need for nerves** ("denervation prevents blastema growth"): kept out so the blastema line stays one idea.
- **Adult fingertips**: "there is evidence that adults can regenerate the soft tissue and nail of the digit tip after amputation, but bone regeneration in adults has not been systematically assessed" (PMC8725625). Not said in the script; Brain's line stays at children.
- **Liver "only organ that regenerates"**: hospital pages say "only organ"; Nature Reviews says "the only solid organ" that returns to 100 %. The script says only that the liver can regrow most of itself.
- Gerald, the axolotl's lost foot and the "thirteen hours" are fiction.
