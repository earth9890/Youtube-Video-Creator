# Episode 88 "What If Gravity Switched Off for Five Seconds?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Tilly, the duck, the lifeguard, the news anchor, Doctor Hale and the Brain are story devices. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published statement or measurement. **Inference** = our arithmetic or reasoning from a solid fact (formulas given, so anyone can redo them). **Story** = a device, never stated as science. Quoted strings are exact as returned by the page; unquoted figures are as the page states them.

Speed at a latitude = equatorial speed x cosine of the latitude; at 45 degrees "roughly 1,184.2 km/h". | Wikipedia, Earth's rotation: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth%27s_rotation |

## Checked and NOT used
- The viral claim that gravity will switch off for several seconds on 12 August: reported by Gathered as a hoax denied by NASA; not used.
- Any claim that the atmosphere would "rush upward" at about 1 g when gravity switches off (a pressure-gradient argument): plausible physics, but no source found; the script says only that the air starts to expand outward, as Science ABC does.
- Any claim about the Earth's rock itself expanding in five seconds: no source; left out.
