## Description
You are not boring. You are just the only one listening to the replay.

Dave talked to Maya at a party for five minutes, then replayed it nine times and decided he was boring. So was he? In 2018, psychologists found that after chatting with a stranger, people rate their partner as more interesting than they think they came across themselves, in the very same conversation. Observers watching the chats could see the liking; the people talking missed it, busy replaying their own mistakes.
