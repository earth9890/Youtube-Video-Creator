# Episode 59: titles and thumbnail ideas

## Titles (pick = 1)
1. **The Pygmalion Effect** (21 chars). The named-effect format (story-explainer.md §5: "The [Name] Effect", unexplained; Zenn's Calhoun Effect 5.0M). The title question ("if you believe a person is about to blossom, can that belief make them blossom?") is spoken at ~0:20-0:26 and answered at ~85 %.
2. Can Expecting More Change Someone? (35). The title question in plain words; good search language for teachers and managers.
3. The Experiment That Lied to Teachers (34). Honest: the researchers told teachers a random list of children were "about to bloom" (the book's own design); strong curiosity hook.
4. Does Believing in Someone Work? (31). Emotional framing for parents, coaches and managers; the video answers "a little, through habits".
5. The Hat That Picked the Smart Kids (33). Playful version of the random-list twist; risks implying the effect was larger than the video shows, so use only as a Test & Compare partner.

All are honest: the video says the effect is real but small and different from the legend, and that the original numbers were attacked.
