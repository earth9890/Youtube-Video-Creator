# Episode 44 "What If You Shrank to the Size of an Ant?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, the shrink ray, its recharge until dinner, his list of dangers, the talking Brain and the talking ant are story devices, not science claims. The premise ("every part of Dave shrank evenly") is stated in the script as a pretend rule. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published statement, measurement or result. **Inference** = our arithmetic or reasoning from a solid fact, kept modest in the script ("on paper", "roughly", "by Haldane's rule").

**Our base numbers (arithmetic, used throughout):** Dave = 1.75 m and 70 kg (story choice). 1.75 m / 5 mm = **350**. Skin (area) scales by 350² = **122,500** ("about a hundred and twenty thousand"). Weight (volume) scales by 350³ = **42,875,000** ("about forty-three million"). 70 kg / 42,875,000 = **1.63 mg** ("less than two milligrams").

## Checked and NOT used
- **Voice and hearing.** Scaling vocal folds by 1/350 would put a 120 Hz voice near 42 kHz, above human hearing and inside a dog's (commonly cited ~45 kHz; not verified). Funny ("only the dog can hear him"), but it stacks two idealised scalings with no direct source; cut.
- **Breathing through tubes.** Haldane on insect tracheae and the diffusion limit ("hardly any insects are much more than half an inch thick") is solid, but tiny Dave's lungs wouldn't be his problem (diffusion is easy at that size), so it answered no question the story asked; cut for length.
- **Jumping.** Haldane: "A flea can jump about two feet, a man about five" (jump height roughly independent of size). True and fun, cut for length.
- **Ant terminal velocity "6.4 km/h" / "4 mph".** Widely repeated, no underlying study found; the script uses our own drag estimate for Dave instead, flagged above.
- **Etruscan shrew "eats 1.5–2 times (or 6–8 times) its body weight".** Encyclopedia-style figures vary; the script uses the lab-colony paper's "more than their body weight" only. (The same paper's crickets-on-offer figure, "about 6 times their body weight", is food offered, not eaten.)
- **"Ants carry 10–50 times their body weight."** Common claim, no study found; the script uses the documented 100× weaver-ant photo.
- **Haldane's "A rat is killed, a man is broken, a horse splashes."** Accurate quote, left out (no gore).
- **"No warm-blooded animal can be smaller than ~2 g."** Often said, but no clean primary source for a hard limit; the script says only that no mammal comes near Dave's size.
- **Haldane's "about half a million" rods and cones per eye.** Outdated (modern counts ~120 million rods, ~6 million cones); not used.
