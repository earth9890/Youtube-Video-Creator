## Description
What if you shrank to the size of an ant? The movies say you'd be too weak, you'd fall off things and you'd get stepped on. Dave is about to find out how wrong the movies are, because the shrink ray needs until dinner to recharge.

At five millimetres, Dave is three hundred and fifty times shorter, about forty-three million times lighter, and on paper three hundred and fifty times stronger. Then comes the part the films leave out: one raindrop that can hold him prisoner, a sunny afternoon that leaves him freezing and starving, and a world made of blur. Along the way: a mouse at the bottom of a mine shaft, mosquitoes in the rain, the smallest mammal on Earth, and the ant who has been doing all of this without effort the whole time.
