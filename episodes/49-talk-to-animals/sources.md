## Checked and NOT used
- **"Prairie dogs describe the size and shape of a person and the colour of clothes"** (a popular summary quoted in a Tucson newspaper): broader than the 2009 paper supports; only the paper's results are used.
- **Honeybee quorum numbers (15 vs 20-30 scouts):** sources conflict; not used.
- **Waggle-dance error margins (plus or minus fifteen degrees):** only seen in a search summary; not used.
- **Commercial "AI pet translators"**: no good evidence found; not used.
- **Earth Species Project / commercial models**: not needed for the story.
- **Parrots, crows, dogs "talking" with buttons:** not enough controlled evidence pulled; not used.
- **Dolphin "names" in the wild beyond the 2013 playback study:** only the 2013 study is cited.

- "Functional reference" is stated as the careful term; the prairie dog result is modest (55 % vs 33 % chance).
- Elephant names: "might have picked up each caller's voice"; names are "one small part".
- Whales: "an alphabet with no dictionary".
- Prize status ("nobody has claimed the grand prize") needs a fresh check before recording.
