## Description
What would really happen if you could talk to animals? Would you learn their secrets, or hear a lot of very short sentences?
Dave wakes up able to understand every animal on Earth. The first thing he hears is a prairie dog describing him: "Tall human. Yellow shirt. Coming this way." Then he climbs from the smallest animal message to the biggest one nobody can read yet: the 2004 shirt experiment with prairie dogs, the bee dance that gives directions to flowers, dolphins and elephants that seem to use names, a border collie who learned 1,022 words and said none of them back, and a sperm whale alphabet with no dictionary. Along the way: what "functional reference" means, why researchers are careful about "meaning", what a ten million dollar prize wants, and the twist hiding under all of it.
