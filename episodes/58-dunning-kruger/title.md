# Episode 58: titles and thumbnail ideas

## Titles (pick = 1)
1. **The Dunning-Kruger Effect** (26 chars). The named-effect format (story-explainer.md §5: "The [Name] Effect"); a huge evergreen search term and a known name, so the "unexplained" format also works as a search title. The video answers the implied question (is it true that the less you know, the surer you are?) at ~85 %.
2. Is Mount Stupid Real? (21). The internet's version of the idea as a yes/no question; the answer is "not as drawn".
3. The Dunning-Kruger Chart Is Fake (32). Honest: the "Mount Stupid" curve is not in the 1999 paper (the video checks the four real graphs). Strong curiosity hook; risk of reading as dunking, so keep as the Test & Compare partner.
4. Do Dumb People Think They're Smart? (34). The popular misreading as a question; the video says that is not what the paper found. Possibly too insulting for the channel tone.
5. The Study Everyone Gets Wrong (28). Myth-bust angle without the name; works if the name alone underperforms.

All are honest: the video says the effect is real but smaller and odder than the chart, and that its size and cause are disputed.

## Thumbnail A: clickbait scene (pick), words "MOUNT STUPID?"
- **Scene:** a bright green hillside against a sky-blue field, sun glow top-left so black ink reads. A giant mountain-shaped chart curve rises behind him, drawn as a thick red line, with a little flag at the peak reading "YOU ARE HERE".
- **Huge Dave in front** (head about 40 % of frame height, s about 4.2), standing proud on the summit, chest out, a lopsided chef's hat, a flat brick-like loaf held up in one hand like a trophy, sparkly proud eyes. Not grinning wildly: smug and sure.
- **The wrongness, big (>= 50 % of frame):** the cliff edge right behind his heels with a tiny sign "CLIFF" and a cloud puff of dust; the audience can see what Dave can't.
- **Small figure:** a tiny calm baker at the bottom of the hill looking up and shaking her head, one hand over her eyes. Three figures at most.
- **Words:** "MOUNT STUPID?" is long (13 chars); use **"STUPID?"** top centre, size 200+, yellow #FEFE06, black halo (haloW 0.09). Alternative: "SURE?". Not the title.
- **Wrongness check:** the video really shows and debunks the chart; the question mark keeps it honest.
