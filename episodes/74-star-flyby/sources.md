## Contested / flagged
- Gliese 710's closest approach has moved with each data release: 13,366 AU (Berski & Dybczynski 2016), 16,000 AU (Bailer-Jones 2018, ESA), 4,303 AU (de la Fuente Marcos 2018), ~10,700 AU (Berski & Dybczynski 2022), ~13,100 AU (Bailer-Jones 2022), ~12,800 AU (a 2026 study). The script says the estimates "keep shuffling" and gives a range.
- Scholz's Star's own distance and date shift with later data (52 kAU at 70 kyr; 68.7 kAU at 80 kyr).
- The Heisler and Tremaine ratio holds on average; individual epochs vary with stochastic stellar passages.
- Comet effects are modelled, not observed: "potentially", "a small number", "at most".
- "Kuiper belt effects negligible" and "magnitude 11.4/-2.7" come from Wikipedia summaries of the literature, not the primary papers.
- The age of our species (300,000 years) is a lower bound of the oldest fossils, with some dispute about attribution.
