# Episode 25 "You Are Not Unlucky": sources (verified 2026-10-14)

Primary source for every Wiseman claim: Richard Wiseman, "The Luck Factor", *Skeptical Inquirer* 27(3), May/June 2003, pp. 26-30. Article page: https://skepticalinquirer.org/2003/05/the-luck-factor/ · PDF: https://centerforinquiry.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2003/05/22164720/p26.pdf · University of Hertfordshire repository copy: https://uhra.herts.ac.uk/id/eprint/2319/ (the article says it "is based on his new book *The Luck Factor*, published in April 2003"). The same material ran in the UK as Wiseman, "Be lucky - it's an easy skill to learn", *The Daily Telegraph*, 9 January 2003.

## Checked and NOT used
- **Luck school "80 % luckier":** reported by Wiseman ("Eighty percent of people were now happier, more satisfied with their lives, and, perhaps most important of all, luckier"), but self-reported after a month, with no control group described. Left out so the video doesn't make a number promise.
- **Four principles** (noticing chance opportunities, intuition, positive expectations, resilience): real, but the video keeps to the first one, which the newspaper test shows.
- **Moving-dot anxiety experiment:** the article cites it without naming the researchers or the numbers ("over a third of them missed the large dots"), so not used.
- **Bank-robbery counterfactual scenario / bronze vs silver medallists:** verified in the same article, cut for length; it belongs to the resilience principle, not this story.
- **Barnett Helzberg meeting Warren Buffett:** the article's opening anecdote; cut, because a real person's anecdote isn't evidence and it isn't our story.
- **Telegraph money figure:** some reprints of the UK version describe the second ad's prize differently; we couldn't fetch the Telegraph original, so the script uses the Skeptical Inquirer's verbatim "$250".
