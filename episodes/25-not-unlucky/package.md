## Description
You are not unlucky. You might just be busy counting.

Nothing good ever happens to Dave, while his friend Sam gets every lucky break. So is Dave cursed? Psychologist Richard Wiseman found that lucky charms did nothing. Then he handed self-described lucky and unlucky people a newspaper and asked them to count the photos. Page two said, in letters over two inches tall, "Stop counting. There are 43 photographs in this newspaper." Halfway through, another ad offered $250. The unlucky people tended to miss both.
