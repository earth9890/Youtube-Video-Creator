# Episode 93 "What If You Lived to 1,000?" (sources, verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Sam, the Lantern Cafe, the cake, the bank manager, HR, the bicycle and the Brain are story devices. The premise (ageing simply stops at thirty-three) is the story's rule card, not a science claim. Everything the narrator or the scientist states as fact is below. **Solid** = published figure. **Inference** = our arithmetic from a solid figure.

## Checked and NOT used
- "Aubrey de Grey says 1,000 years": the thousand-year figure is used only via Vaupel's published estimate, not any one person's claim.
- Longevity-medicine claims (life extension drugs, caloric restriction): not used; the premise is the rule card.
- The "Hydra never ages" claim: an interesting animal, left out for length.
- Dolly / telomeres: covered in episode 92.

## Unsure / flagged
- 57 % depends on a constant 1-in-1,743 risk for 967 years; real risk changes (cars get safer, new hazards). The script says "better than a coin flip, slightly".
- Methuselah's exact age is debated (4,669 to 4,858 depending on who is asked); the script says "more than four thousand eight hundred" (Guinness). Consider changing to "nearly five thousand" if the owner prefers.
- "A thousand-year-old bank" gag uses two 550-year banks as arithmetic.
