# Episode 71: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **What If You Fell Into Jupiter?** (31 chars). "What If...?" shape. The title question is spoken at ~0:20 and answered at ~86 %. Planet-sized "what if" topics sit in the same family as the channel's black-hole fall.
2. What Happens If You Fall Into Jupiter? (38). The plainer search phrasing.
3. Could You Land on Jupiter? (26). Honest and short: the answer is the twist (there is nothing to land on).
4. What Is Inside Jupiter? (23). Too broad; it covers only the last third.
5. The Planet With No Ground (26). Intriguing, but it gives away the answer; keep for a Short.
