## Checked and NOT used
- **"Hibernation reduces ageing / extends lifespan."** Greenberg mentions "possibly extended lifespan" as a goal; speculative, not used.
- **Bears and muscle loss** beyond ESA's planning assumption: no primary source pulled; not stated about bears.
- **NASA / SpaceWorks torpor study** (90-180 days): only found in a repost on an unrelated site; not used.
- **Choukér et al. 2021** (5-7 months torpor, "AI assisted monitoring"): only seen via search summary; replaced by the ESA article's own wording about autonomous operation.
- **Heart-rate figure for bears:** National Geographic says 55 to 9, ScienceDaily says 55 to 14; we use 14 and flag the disagreement here.
- **Tail-fat share of body weight ("up to 40 %")** (Wikipedia): weak source; not used.
- **"Lemurs do not interrupt hibernation with spontaneous arousals"** (Dausmann 2005, seen only in a search summary): omitted; the 2016 paper's REM-like-states result is used instead.
- **Why squirrels' blood does not freeze** (supercooling mechanism): PBS says the mechanism is unknown; not used.

## Contested / flagged in the script
- The Spanish-cave hibernation idea: stated as an argument, with critics named in the next line.
- Wake-ups "to sleep": stated as "one idea", followed by "mixed results" and "it remains unclear".
- Bear heart rate 14 vs 9: we use 14 (ScienceDaily).
