## Checked and NOT used
- "Oldest mirrors" at Çatalhöyük being definitively "the earliest": sources (Wikipedia/Melchior-Bonnet, popular articles) say so but no authoritative excavation report was checked; the script says "oldest manufactured mirrors anyone has found" and "as far as anyone knows".
- Mellaart's claim of eight mirrors in graves / "funerary mirrors": secondary and disputed (Mellaart's burial interpretations are disputed by later work); not used.
- Versailles Hall of Mirrors (357 mirrors) and its cost: no reliable price found; not used. The "poisoned Murano workers" legend: unverified; not used.
- Colbert smuggling Venetian workers (1664): reported in popular sources but not confirmed in a primary source; the script says only that the secret "leaked".
- Edmund Carpenter's famous "Biami are terrified of mirrors" story: widely repeated, not verified, so not used.
- Seneca on Hostius Quadra's magnifying mirrors: not checked in time; not used.
- Octopus-style animal mirror results (cleaner wrasse, magpies): not needed.
- Broesch ages: Kenya sample 18-72 months, other sites 36-55 months: kept out of the script's numbers to avoid implying a direct comparison; mentioned here.
