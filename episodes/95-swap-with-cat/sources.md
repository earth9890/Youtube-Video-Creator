# Episode 95 "What If You Swapped Bodies With Your Cat for a Day?" (sources, verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Biscuit, Maisie, the interview panel, the doughnuts and the Brain are story devices. The body-swap itself is the rule card (you each keep your own head), so Biscuit's thoughts are invented; everything about what a cat's BODY can do (hear, see, taste, sleep, fall, purr) is real. **Solid** = published fact. **Inference** = our reasoning.

- Cat sleep "crepuscular" detail (VCA): true but not needed for the story.
