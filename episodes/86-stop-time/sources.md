# Episode 86 "What If You Could Stop Time?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Ivy, the grandad watch, Doctor Hale's voice note, the Brain and the "arm's length bubble" are story devices. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published statement or measurement. **Inference** = our arithmetic or reasoning from a solid fact. **Story** = a device, never stated as science. Quoted strings are exactly as returned by the page; unquoted figures are as the page states them (the fetch tool returns short quotes and paraphrases).

## Checked and NOT used
- The Odyssey Online's claim that stopping photons would make atoms fly apart: a blog, and a conflation of virtual photons with light; not used.
- Futurism's claims that you would freeze to death and could not breathe: plausible within the thought experiment but unsourced; the rule card hands you breathable air instead.
- New Scientist's Last Word answer ("Nothing", because every force acts over time): a good caveat that stopping time everywhere is not physically meaningful. The script handles it with "In this story" and "Nothing else changes", and a line in the video description should say the stopped-time premise is fiction.
- The Worldbuilding Stack Exchange answer (everything would act like a mirror): interesting, but a forum and one of several options; not used.
