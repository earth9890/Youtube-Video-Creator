# Episode 86: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **What If You Could Stop Time?** (28 chars). The question the script speaks at ~0:35; the answer (blind, deaf, syrup) is the hook.
2. What If You Stopped Time for Eight Minutes? (43). Puts the clock into the title.
3. What If You Paused the World? (30). Plainer, closer to the button.
4. What If You Could Stop Time? You'd Go Blind (43). States the twist; use if the first underperforms.
5. What If You Pressed Pause on Time? (35). The PAUSE button as the hook.

## Thumbnails (thumbnail-designer.md: clickbait scene mode for A, old style for B)

**A, clickbait scene (pick).**
- Scene: a dim blue-black street at dusk; a pigeon hanging in the air at the top of the frame like a sticker, a bus behind with a puff of exhaust frozen in place, everything lit by a single warm circle of light on the ground.
- Front: a huge Dave (head about 40 % of the frame height) in the middle of the circle of light, both arms stretched out in front of him pushing on nothing, eyes wide, cheeks puffed out as if pushing a wall of air. A small phone in his pocket glows with the number 9 %.
- The one wrongness: Dave is leaning on a wall made of air, and a visible wall-shaped ripple of air hangs in front of him.
- Words: **CAN'T SEE?** (9 characters) in yellow #FEFE06 with a black halo, top-left, size about 200. It is the viewer's reaction, not the title.
- Honest hook: the video really is about being blind and wading through air when time stops.
