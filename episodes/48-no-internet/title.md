# Episode 48: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **What If the Internet Disappeared for a Week?** (43 chars) — the spoken question; a universal, huge-stakes premise; the real answer is surprisingly physical (sea cables, the Sun).
2. What If the Internet Just Stopped? (35) — shorter; drops the "week" hook.
3. The Week the Internet Went Away (32) — "The week…" story framing; less question-led.
4. Could the Sun Switch Off the Internet? (37) — gives the twist; strong curiosity and search.
5. Where Does the Internet Actually Live? (38) — the "wet wire" chapter; risks losing the what-if.
