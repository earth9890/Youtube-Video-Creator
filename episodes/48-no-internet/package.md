## Description
What if the internet disappeared for a week? Would the world carry on, or would everything quietly stop?
Dave unplugs his router, feels free, and then the whole internet goes away. Everywhere. For one week.

Each day Dave loses something and we look at a real case where it already happened: the sea-floor cables that carry almost all intercontinental data (and how one volcano cut a whole country off for five weeks), a single data-centre failure that stopped five million card payments, a country that switched its internet off and lost about eighteen million dollars a day, hospitals locked out of their own computers, and a shipping giant rebuilt by hand. Along the way we meet the one thing that keeps working without the internet (GPS, and the clocks the world borrows from it), then the one realistic way the whole internet could really go away: the Sun. The twist is that a week is the gentle version.
