# Episode 57 "The Ringelmann Effect": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Read in full text: Kravitz & Martin 1986 (the paper that found Ringelmann's original report; PDF), Latané, Williams & Harkins 1979 (PDF, MIT copy; it also reports Ingham et al. 1974 in detail). Read in abstract: Williams, Harkins & Latané 1981; Karau & Williams 1993. Dave, the physicist and the picnic, "forty-nine percent on the gauge", the accounting team, the group project and "ninety-one percent" are invented story devices. We could not open Ingham et al. 1974 itself; its numbers are quoted as Latané et al. 1979 report them.

## Checked and NOT used
- **"63 kilograms alone, 160 for three, 248 for eight."** Latané et al. 1979 print these as Ringelmann's rope results; Kravitz & Martin show the table they come from has no stated task and gives only relative figures. Not used.
- **"Ringelmann was a German psychologist who never published."** The 1979 paper's own claim and a widespread textbook error; the script presents it as the wrong turn, citing Kravitz & Martin 1986.
- **A secondary text (forrt.org open social psychology) says Ingham et al. blindfolded volunteers pulling "groups of up to five" and calls it a paradigm shift.** Used Latané et al.'s description instead.
- **Gender and culture results ("loafing less for women and in Eastern cultures").** Only "culture" is in the abstract; gender is from secondary summaries, so the script says "what culture they come from" only.
- **"Loafing even when unaware" (Karau & Williams via a Wikipedia summary).** Not verified in the paper itself; not used.
- **Anti-Ringelmann ants (army ants, weaver ants).** Wikipedia only; fun but off-spine and not verified.
- **Kibbutz vs kolkhoz in Latané et al. 1979 (private plots produce 21 % of Soviet farm output).** Cold-war-era figures cited second-hand; cut.
- **Ringelmann's 1883 26-series (cart pushing, wheelbarrow, dyads pushing less than the sum, p = .027).** Interesting but off the rope spine; not used.
