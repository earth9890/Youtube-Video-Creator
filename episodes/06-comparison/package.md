## Description
Students who cut social media to half an hour a day felt less lonely and less depressed within three weeks.
Here's why your feed makes you feel small, and the three-step fix you can do today.

You're comparing your behind-the-scenes to everyone else's highlight reel. You see the vacation, the promotion, the perfect morning, never the doubt, the debt or the delete button. And the people furthest ahead of you don't inspire you; they crush you. This video gives you a time limit, an unfollow list, and the one person worth comparing yourself to.
