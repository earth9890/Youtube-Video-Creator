# Episode 91 "What If You Could Photosynthesize?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Poppy, Mr Fenwick, Mum, the shed, the baking tray, the barbecue, the bet and the Brain are story devices, not science claims. Everything the narrator or the scientist states as fact is below. **Solid** = published measurement or figure. **Inference** = our arithmetic or plain geometry from a solid figure, kept modest in the script. Setting: a July day in London (the script never names the city; the July/December figures below are London's).

## Checked and NOT used
- Pea aphids and other animals that make their own pigments or hold algae: not needed.
- Claims that the sea slug took algal genes into its own DNA (horizontal gene transfer): contested in the literature; the Green et al. 2000 abstract says no algal nuclear genome was detected in the slug. Left out.
- Daily sunshine "5-6 kWh/m2" for sunny places: NREL figures vary widely by site; we used London's published July/December values so the tennis-court comparison has one traceable source.
- The 4.6 % / 6 % theoretical maximum: true (Zhu et al. 2008) but would make Dave look better than the real-crop record; the script uses the achieved record so the "best case" is honest.

## Unsure / flagged
- The 8 %, 21 m2 and "singles court" figures are our arithmetic on horizontal-surface sunshine; a standing body gets sun on a different angle. The script presents them as a thought-experiment best case.
- "Twelve watts" uses the UBC estimate of 0.5 m2 facing the sun for a standing person (their assumption, not a measured average).
