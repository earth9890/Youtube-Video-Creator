# Episode 92 "What If You Cloned Yourself?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, the clone (Arlo / "Dave Two"), Gran, Mr Pugh, the boat, the baker and the Brain are story devices, not science claims. In real life no human has been cloned; the script says so and the rule card makes cloning "work first time" for the story. Everything the narrator or the scientist states as fact is below. **Solid** = published fact. **Inference** = our framing of a solid fact.

## Checked and NOT used
- That Dolly's early death proves clones age faster: contested. Nature's obituary: "There were no other signs of premature ageing"; Roslin scientists saw no link; Sinclair 2016 found healthy aged clones. The script shows both the scare and the relief.
- Mitchell/other "personality is X % genes": the single 49 % figure is the only heritability number used.
- Any pet-cloning price or company: not needed, no brands named.
- A person's life expectancy figure for the sting: left out so the sting needs no extra source.

## Unsure / flagged
- "Thirty-two years" depends on Dave's assumed age (32), a story device.
- The sheep lifespan: AVMA/Roslin say 11-12; Sinclair says "typically <10". The sting uses "eleven or twelve".
- The clone "nearly your voice" is a story gag; voices are partly set by genes and partly by anatomy and habit; no source claimed.
