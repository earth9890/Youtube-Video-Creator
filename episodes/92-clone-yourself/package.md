## Description
What would really happen if you cloned yourself? Would you get a copy of today's you, or a twin who arrives thirty-two years late?
Dave wants to finish a family boat for Gran's ninetieth birthday, so he clones himself to build it. The rule of this story: the cloning works first time. Everything after that is just biology. Follow the baby from day one to eighteen: why a clone's fingerprints don't match, what the first cloned cat's coat teaches us, what fifty years of twin studies say about genes, what Dolly the sheep's six years and her copies' healthy old age really showed, and how twins drift apart as they grow up.
