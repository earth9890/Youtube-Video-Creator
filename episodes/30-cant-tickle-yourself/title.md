## Thumbnail A (floor-raiser, archetype A): "0.2 SECONDS?"
- **Image:** big-head Dave cropped on the left (s ≈ 4.3, head ≈ 40 % of frame height), flat deadpan face, eyes sideways in mild unease, his own hand holding a feather against his ribs, with zero reaction. On the right, filling the half: the strange object, a chunky red box with a big dial labelled "DELAY", the needle on 0.2 s, its cable running to the feather.
- **Background:** two flat fields: warm orange #FCA55D wall, navy #202C36 floor band. Three elements: Dave, the feather hand, the delay box.
- **Text:** "0.2 SECONDS?" in the top-right band, yellow #FEFE06 with a black outline (haloW 0.09). It doesn't repeat the title; it promises the strange answer (the hook's planted number). Alternatives: "STILL NOTHING", "TOO EARLY?".

## Thumbnail B (lottery ticket, archetype H, literal idiom): "CANCELLED"
- **Image:** near-black #05031A. One big white doodle hand (thick outline) holding a feather, centred-left, ≤ 45 % of the width. Across the hand, a slightly tilted red rubber-stamp mark that reads **CANCELLED**, as if stamped by the brain. Nothing else on the frame.
- **Why:** it is the twist ("your brain is constantly cancelling you out of your own life") drawn literally, one stakes word, one red mark. The title supplies the question; the stamp supplies a darker answer the viewer didn't expect from a tickling video.
- **Text rule:** the stamp is the only text (1 word, red #E01E1E, no outline on black).
