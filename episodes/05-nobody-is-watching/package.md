## Description
You feel like the whole room notices every awkward thing you do. A study says 23% did.
This is the spotlight effect — and once you see it, you stop living for an audience that isn't there.

Researchers made students wear an embarrassing T-shirt into a crowded room; they guessed half the room would notice, but only 23% did. Everyone is the main character of their own story, too busy with their own spotlight to track yours. So ask the question, share the idea, walk into the gym — the spotlight gets smaller every time you do.
