## Checked and NOT used
- **Error counts per trial (about 30 on trial one, about 5-6 by trial ten):** appear on Wikipedia; I could not tie them to Milner's 1962 paper, so the script uses Squire's wording (ten trials, retention across three days).
- **"Surgery on September 1, 1953" vs "August 25":** sources disagree; the script says "in nineteen fifty-three".
- **"Eight centimetres removed":** the script says the surgeon's drawings showed it, and the scan later showed a little over five; it never says what exactly was removed.
- **Livestream "viewers donated":** Annese's acknowledgements mention contributions from viewers of the web broadcast; not needed.
- **Pronunciation of "Molaison":** not verified; flagged in the script header.
- **Scoville's retractors causing the frontal lesion:** a speculation on Wikipedia; not stated.
- **Corkin 2002 (Nat Rev Neurosci) text:** paywalled; only the abstract and references read ("nearly 100 investigators, first at the Montreal Neurological Institute and since 1966 at MIT"); the cut line about "nearly a hundred researchers" was dropped for length.
