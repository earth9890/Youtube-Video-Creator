# Episode 84 "What If You Had a Dog's Nose?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Gran, Grandpa's coat, Uncle Roy, Tilly, Pudding, the cake, the cardigan and "you do not get a tail" are story devices, not science claims. Everything the narrator or the cast states as fact is below. **Solid** = published source. **Inference** = our reasoning from a solid fact.

## Checked and NOT used
- Dogs detecting COVID-19 (Jendrny et al. 2020, 94 % overall detection in a pilot; later trials 82-94 % sensitivity): real, but one disease is enough and the malaria study is the more peer-reviewed example. Not used.
- "Dogs smell fear" and "dogs smell time" as pop-science claims: only the footstep-direction result is used.
- Olfactory receptor gene counts (dog about 811 vs human about 396 functional): the receptor-cell numbers from NOVA are used instead.
- "Dogs are 10,000 to 100,000 times more sensitive": used in episode 83's sting; not repeated here.
