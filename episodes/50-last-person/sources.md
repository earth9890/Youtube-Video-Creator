# Episode 50 "What If You Were the Last Person on Earth?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, his diary, the lamp, the fire and the dog are story devices (the dog is not a prediction about any person). The "time-lapse" of the world without people is **Alan Weisman's projection** in *The World Without Us* (2007), built from interviews with engineers and scientists. The script says so ("Weisman's projection", "he expected", "he says"). The real-world tests (Cyprus, the Korean DMZ, Pripyat and Chernobyl) are the check. **Solid** = published statement or measurement. **Projection** = Weisman's reasoned estimate, stated as his. **Inference** = ours.

## Checked and NOT used
- **Nuclear plants failing within a year** ("every nuclear power plant on Earth would have failed or melted down"): this is Weisman's claim as summarised by NPR; other estimates conflict (one source says one to two weeks) and it is unsettling and contested. Not used.
- **How long power plants run before shutting down**, and what hydro, wind and solar do: no strong source found; the script does not claim a timeline for the lights beyond Weisman's "the power would go off".
- **Hour-by-hour timelines** from other books and documentaries: low-quality summaries only; not used.
- **Weisman on Varosha by name**: the NPR transcript calls it "another demilitarized zone in Cyprus"; the script does not name the town.
- **Pet-specific claims** ("dogs would fall prey to predators", "cats win"): found only in reviews; replaced by the cat and dog numbers above.
- **The year-by-year state of Chernobyl's wildlife since 2015**, and of the dog population: not updated; the 2015 census is cited as such.
- **Real people**: Weisman and the cited scientists are named only for their published statements; no one else.

## Contested / flagged in the script
- Everything about the future is labelled Weisman's projection ("he says", "he expected").
- The dog share: "roaming free is not the same as living without people".
- Chernobyl: the team's own caveat about radiation is included.
