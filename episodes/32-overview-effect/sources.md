## Checked and NOT used
- **Gagarin's "Circling the Earth... I marveled at the beauty of our planet. People, let us preserve and increase this beauty, not destroy it!"** Widely quoted (Wikipedia carries it), but I could not trace it to a primary document; used his State Commission answers (Wilson Center archive) instead. Same for "I see Earth. It is so beautiful."
- **Artemis II (1-10 April 2026):** flew round the Moon, set a new distance record (406,771 km) and photographed an earthset and earthrise; 28 people have now travelled to the Moon's vicinity. Real and fresh, but a second Moon story would crowd the Earthrise section. Good for the description or pinned comment. Christina Koch's line "every single person that you know is sustained and inside of that green line" comes from a NASA publication of November 2025 about the ISS, not from Artemis II itself.
- **Fred Hoyle, 1948/1950:** "a new idea as powerful as any in history will be let loose" (*The Nature of the Universe*, 1950, from lectures in the preceding years) and 1979: "only an hour's drive away if your car could go straight upwards" (The Observer, 9 Sep 1979). Both verified; cut for length.
- **"Earthrise started the environmental movement / Earth Day":** commonly claimed (Galen Rowell: "the most influential environmental photograph ever taken"), but contested; others credit *Silent Spring* (1962) and 1960s disasters. Not claimed.
- **Shatner "the oldest person in space":** true in October 2021, but Ed Dwight (90 years 8 months, NS-25, May 2024) later took the record. The script says only "aged ninety".
- **Blue Origin ticket price:** never published. Used Virgin Galactic's public $450,000 instead.
- **Who pressed the Blue Marble shutter:** NASA credits the crew; probably Harrison Schmitt. Not needed.
- **Earthrise's own rotation for release:** the Upside Down project says it was rotated 135 degrees. Not checked against NASA; not used.
- **Michael Collins ("tiny, shiny... fragile"), Ed Gibson, Ed Dwight, Cai Xuzhe, Jeremy Hansen ("Moon joy") quotes:** real, but enough voices already.
- **Bai et al. 2017 (Yosemite tourists drew themselves smaller):** a nice "small self" study; overlaps with Piff and Sturm, cut for length.
- **Joe Allen to White: "I thought it was an extension of my travel on the Earth."** Supports the twist; cut for length.
- **2018 survey of 39 astronauts (humanistic over spiritual changes):** not used.
- **Yaden/Eichstaedt VR plans:** a lab follow-up was planned in 2016; not used.
- **Virgin Galactic flight status in 2026:** flights paused in mid-2024 to build Delta-class ships; resumption plans for 2026 were not verified, so the script says only that seats "have sold".
- **Anders' death in a 2024 plane crash:** true, and left out on purpose (kind tone, not relevant).

## Notes on contested or soft claims
- "Fewer than eight hundred": the count depends on where space begins (80 km vs 100 km), and every count is under 800.
- "probably one of the most shared photos ever taken" and "one of the most famous photographs of the century" are hedged on purpose. No exact count exists.
