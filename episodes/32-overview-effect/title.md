# Episode 32: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **The Overview Effect** (19 chars). A named effect, left unexplained, like Zenn's "The Calhoun Effect". The video says the name at ~0:12 and explains it by the end.
2. Why Astronauts Come Back Different (34)
3. What Seeing Earth From Space Does to You (40)
4. Why Seeing Earth From Space Changes People (42)
5. Do You Need Space to Feel the Overview Effect? (46)

None of these promises more than the video delivers. The video says the full effect is from space, and that you can get a small dose on the ground (one small study).

## Thumbnail ideas (thumbnail-designer.md: one wrongness, ≤ 3 elements, space = ≥ 40 % near-black)

**A. Floor-raiser (archetype A on a space background): "the planet has a price tag"**
- Near-black #05031A over ≥ 50 % of the frame, with a few faint stars at most.
- On the left, Dave, big (head ≥ 25 % of the height), white outline, eyebrows sceptical, eyes looking sideways at the Earth. He holds his small glass jar labelled SPACE FUND, with three coins in it.
- On the right, a glowing Blue Marble Earth fills about 40 % of the width, with a red #D93A45 paper price tag on a string: "$450,000".
- Yellow #FEFE06 text with a thick black outline across the top: **"ONLY FROM SPACE?"** (3 words). It turns the sales pitch's "only" into a question, and it is not the title.
- Elements: Dave with jar, Earth with tag, text = 3. Wrongness: a price tag on the planet.

**B. Lottery ticket (archetype B: one symbol on black, no text): "the Earth in an eye"**
- Pure black frame. One huge stick-figure eye, centred, ≤ 45 % of the width: a white ring, plus one glint.
- The pupil is the tiny pale-blue Earth, with a single thin diagonal sunbeam streak crossing it (the Pale Blue Dot's ray).
- The only accent is cyan #30B0E9. No words, and no title words in the image (named-phenomenon rule: Calhoun's yellow eye went x42).
- Wrongness: a whole planet sitting in a person's eye, which is the overview effect made literal.

Both need the `thumb_check.py --space` feed-grid test before shipping.
