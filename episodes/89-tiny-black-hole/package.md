## Description
What would happen if you found a tiny black hole in your pocket? Could you keep it, or would it pull your keys, your hat and the bus queue into orbit?
Dave finds something small and dark in a jumble-sale coat, with a mint, a coin and his keys circling it. Lost Property closes at five, so he has seventy minutes and one biscuit tin to hand it in. On the way: why Stephen Hawking said tiny black holes could have formed in the first second of the universe, how a hole that weighs about a billion tonnes can be smaller than an atom, why a black hole glows (and why the smaller it is, the hotter), what a mini black hole would do within two and a half metres, why you can never put it down, and the one place on Earth that can hold it.
