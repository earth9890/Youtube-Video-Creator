Sources:

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, American Time Use Survey 2025: on weekends, 55 minutes a day socialising and 3.3 hours watching TV
- WHO Commission on Social Connection (2025): 1 in 6 people worldwide is affected by loneliness
- Liu, Rim, Min & Min (2022), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, "The surprise of reaching out": people underestimate how much others appreciate hearing from them
