## Description
You are not lazy. Lazy people don't scrub ovens.

Dave finally sat down to paint, then cleaned the oven instead. So what dragged him away? Not his phone, and not laziness. In 2001, psychologists upset a group of students and left them alone with a video game before a test. Then they told some of them that a scented candle had frozen their mood. It was just a candle, but those students stopped goofing off. Procrastination isn't avoiding work. It's avoiding a feeling, and that feeling shrinks once you start.
