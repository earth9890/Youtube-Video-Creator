# Episode 90 "What If You Could Run at the Speed of Light?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, Arlo, the jam jar, grandad's watch, the rocket, the slippers, Doctor Hale and the Brain are story devices. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published statement or measurement. **Inference** = our arithmetic from published values (formulas and inputs given; computed 2026-10-22 in Python). **Story** = a device, never stated as science. Quoted strings are exact as returned by the page; unquoted figures are as the page states them.

Working numbers: v = 0.99 c; Lorentz factor gamma = 1 / sqrt(1 - v^2/c^2) = 7.089 (Wikipedia, Lorentz factor, table: "0.990 ... 7.089"; 0.999 gives 22.366; 0.9999 gives 70.7, our calculation from the same definition).

## Checked and NOT used
- "Time stops at the speed of light" (a photon "experiences no time"): true only as a limit that cannot be applied to a person; the script uses the dilation factor at 99 % instead.
- A "relativistic mass" framing: avoided, since modern texts prefer kinetic energy (gamma - 1) m c^2.
- Numbers for the blue shift of the Sun's light ahead of a 99 % traveller (not sourced); the script says only "shifted towards blue".
- Any specific figure for the blast of the garden: Munroe's own result is for a baseball at 0.9 c, not a person at 0.99 c; the script says only "a very bright white ball" and "Please do not try this at home".
- The LHC page at CERN (bot wall, 403/Anubis): the 99.9999991 % figure comes from search summaries of the CERN Bulletin and the CERN Facts page; re-check before putting the figure in a Short.
