## Checked and NOT used
- Exact date of Röntgen's first picture: sources disagree (8 Nov vs 22 Dec 1895), so only "eighteen ninety-five" is spoken.
- "FDA banned shoe-fitting fluoroscopes in 1953" (Wikipedia timeline): the FDA's authority at that date is doubtful and the ORAU museum page does not say it; not used. We use ORAU's Pennsylvania 1957.
- Backscatter X-ray scanners (security): real, show surface objects rather than bones; not used, the rule card covers it.
- Eye-lens sensitivity to radiation and cataract thresholds: not verified this pass; the script makes no claim about Dave's own eyes.
- Whether the doctor's "ionizing" line should name an exposure time for hospital pictures: left out on purpose (no number).
