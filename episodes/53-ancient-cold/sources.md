## Checked and NOT used
- Neanderthal "nasal cavity as cold adaptation" was historically contested; the 2025 review now supports it, so the script says "long considered a puzzle, now looks well suited".
- "Neanderthals were the first early humans to wear clothing" (Smithsonian page): not used; it conflicts with the Hallett (Africa, 120-90k) and louse evidence.
- Wikipedia's Mezhyrich "149 mammoth bones, 15,000 years": superseded by Chu 2025 ("hundreds" of bones, ~18,000 years); not used.
- Denisova "50,000-year-old needle": press-only dating; not used (see table).
- Gavan's undergraduate essay (body shape not explained by cold alone) and the GVSU grant abstract (limb proportions may raise walking cost): lesser sources; the 2025 peer-reviewed review is used instead.
- Last Glacial Maximum "Europe was abandoned" claims: not checked; the script does not make them.
- Mezhyrich mammoths "hunted vs scavenged": no source found; not claimed.
- Weaver 2003 PNAS (Neanderthal femur as a "hyperpolar" body form): not needed.
