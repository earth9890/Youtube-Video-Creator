# What If We Live Inside a Black Hole? — fun version (~200 words, ~65 s)

1. Your house has walls, a roof, and Wi-Fi that only works in one corner.
2. What it might not have... is an exit.
3. Because some scientists think our whole universe is sitting inside a black hole.
4. Which honestly explains a lot. Mondays. Traffic. Your electricity bill.
5. Quick recap: a black hole eats everything. Stars. Planets. Light. Your weekend plans.
6. Once you're in, you can't get out. Like the family WhatsApp group.
7. And some physicists say: fall in deep enough, and boom, a brand new universe on the other side.
8. Like a cosmic Kinder Joy. Surprise! It's us.
9. So would we feel it? Nope. A black hole this big wouldn't even squish you. You'd still have to go to work.
10. Even the galaxies look suspicious. Way too many of them spin the same way. Like the universe was born on a merry-go-round.
11. Other scientists say: relax, it's probably nothing. Science is mostly "maybe" in a lab coat.
12. But if it's true, every black hole up there could have a whole universe inside.
13. With its own people, in their own houses, with their own bad Wi-Fi.
14. Looking up, wondering if they live in a black hole.
15. Spoiler: they do. Wave hi. They're probably stuck in traffic too.
