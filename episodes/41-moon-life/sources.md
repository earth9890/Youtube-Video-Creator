# Episode 41 "What If You Tried to Live on the Moon?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, his suitcase and packing list, the jumper, the frozen bottle, the TV anchor and Mission Control are story devices, not science claims. Dave's timeline (hour one, day four, sunset "around day fifteen") assumes he arrives at sunrise near the equator on the near side. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published measurement or result. **Inference** = our reasoning or arithmetic from a solid fact, kept modest in the script.

## Checked and NOT used
- "Lunar day temperatures +127 °C / −173 °C" (NASA's 2022 pit release and Diviner's ~100 K night). True, but we use NASA's public "Weather on the Moon" figures (121 °C / −133 °C) to match the brief's range; both are noted above.
- "2.6 times the ISS dose": the paper's ratio is against the ISS's cosmic-ray component only; replaced with the total-vs-total "nearly twice".
- The 2018 M3 confirmation of surface ice (Li et al. PNAS, about 3.5 % of cold traps show exposed ice): solid, cut for length.
- LCROSS's "about 5.6–6 % water by mass" in Cabeus: solid, cut for length.
- Chang'e 4's radiation detector switching off every lunar night to save its batteries (Kiel 2020): solid, cut for length.
- Apollo-era "kangaroo hop" quote from Buzz Aldrin (ALSJ): solid, but no astronaut is named in the script (the three Apollo lines are unnamed paraphrases).
- The ESA/Trento figures for the cave (entrance "at least 55 metres wide", "75 metres deep" overhang walls, ESA's blog): sources differ on width and length, so the script uses only the paper's "tens of metres".
- The Apollo 11 regolith being the most stressful to plants (more "mature" soil): solid, cut for length.
- NASA ICES 2021 paper (Jones): "humans have had only two weeks of exposure to lunar gravity" and a model predicting 0.39 % bone loss a week in lunar gravity: a model, not a measurement; not used.
- Moon dust and long-term lung toxicity ("research shows that lunar soil simulants can destroy lung and brain cells", ESA): real but unsettled and scarier than needed; not used.
- Earth's phases seen from the Moon and earthshine: true, but close to ep31's "night-light" territory and off the spine; not used.
- Tides, the Moon moving away, Apollo retroreflectors, Theia: covered in ep31; deliberately not repeated.
