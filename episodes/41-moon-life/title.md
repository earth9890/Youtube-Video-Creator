# Episode 41: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **What If You Tried to Live on the Moon?** (38 chars): the title question is spoken at ~0:10 and answered in the last chapter ("on it, a fight; under it, you might manage"). It's Zenn's "What If…?" shape, and "live on the Moon" is a big evergreen search (Artemis keeps it in the news).
2. Could You Actually Live on the Moon? (36): the myth-bust "Actually"; strong search, but a weaker story hook than "you tried".
3. What If You Moved to the Moon With One Suitcase? (48): the hook image itself; more specific, slightly long.
4. Why Nobody Has Stayed on the Moon Longer Than 3 Days (52): true (Apollo 17's 75 hours), curiosity gap; but it only covers the first two minutes.
5. The Best Home on the Moon Is a Hole (35): true (the pit at 17 °C), but it gives away the twist.
