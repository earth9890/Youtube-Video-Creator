## Description
You are not weird. You're just the first one to say it.

Dave stands in the corner at every party, sure he's the weird one, so he plans to copy Kevin, the normal one. But where is the normal person? In 1950 the US Air Force measured 4,063 pilots to find the average one and found zero. And when Princeton students were asked how comfortable they were with campus drinking, they said, on average, "less than the average student", who turned out to be them.
