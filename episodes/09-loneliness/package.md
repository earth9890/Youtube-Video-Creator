## Description
About half of all adults say they feel lonely — and loneliness can harm your health as much as smoking 15 cigarettes a day.
If you're surrounded by people and still feel alone, this video shows the one small thing that breaks the loop tonight.

A short whiteboard story about why loneliness has nothing to do with how many people you know, and everything to do with feeling seen by one of them.
We look at the cruel part — how loneliness makes you read rejection where there is none, so you pull back and get lonelier — and three tiny moves out: send one real message, say yes to one invitation, ask one person how they're really doing.
You're not too much. You're not too little. You're just not yet in the right room.
