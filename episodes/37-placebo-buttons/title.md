# Episode 37: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick = 1)
1. **Why You Press Buttons That Do Nothing** (37 chars). "Why you…" format; true as stated (the buttons in the video really do nothing for the presser's goal), and it carries both halves of the script: the dead buttons and why we keep pressing.
2. The Placebo Button Effect (25). "The X Effect" format; the NYT's own phrase was "mechanical placebos".
3. Why Fake Buttons Still Work (27). States the twist; "still work" is honest (they work on the presser, per Glass & Singer), but it gives the ending away.
4. Why New York's Walk Buttons Do Nothing (38). Precise but narrow; loses the lift, thermostat and psychology.
5. Does the Door-Close Button Do Anything? (39). Strong single-object question; the honest answer ("not for you, yes for firefighters") is one section, not the video.

Not used: "Why Most Buttons Don't Do Anything" (the brief's direction). Most buttons do work; the evidence covers specific buttons only, and the script's last lines say so.
