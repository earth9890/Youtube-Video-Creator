## Checked and NOT used
- **"Fedwire's biggest single transfer is $9,999,999,999.99"**: a payments-API doc says so, but the FDIC exam module says "$1 to $999,999,999.99" (one cent under a billion). The conflict is unresolved, so the script never says the billion went in one transfer ("he sends the billion").
- **Most expensive painting sold ($450.3 M, 2017)**: Guinness confirms the record as of Feb 2024, but I couldn't confirm it still stands in Oct 2026, and it adds little; cut.
- **2024 $1 billion gift that made a New York medical school tuition-free "in perpetuity"** (NPR): true and kind, but "in perpetuity" works because the gift is invested, the opposite of the "spend it" rule; cut.
- **Medians for credit card limits and supermarket checkout speeds**: couldn't source cleanly; Dave's card limit and trolley stay story devices.
- **"The average cash home sale closes in N days"**: sources only agree on "one to two weeks"; the 37-day ICE figure is for all (mostly mortgaged) closings, so it isn't used.
- **Fedwire 9 pm reopening**: real (the next business day opens at 9:00 p.m. ET), but a payment then counts toward the next business day; leaving it out keeps the 6:45 deadline honest.
- **Multiplier as "the money multiplies"**: avoided; the script gives CBO's range (0.5 to 2.5) and says plainly it is argued about.
