## Description
At one minute past midnight, Dave wins a billion dollars with one rule: spend it all by the next midnight, or whatever's left vanishes. How hard can it be? Twenty minutes into a supermarket trolley, he is fourteen million dollars behind, and by lunch he is looking at a hundred thousand government forms. So what would really happen if you had to spend a billion dollars in a day?

Dave runs into the maths (about $11,574 every second), the weight of cash (ten million notes, about ten tonnes, roughly two big elephants), the cash-reporting rule that turns a car showroom into paperwork, houses that take a week or two to close, what a billion buys by itself (thirteen rocket launches, a tenth of a space telescope, one $920 million hospital), twelve cents for everyone on Earth, and a deadline that isn't midnight: the Federal Reserve's wire system stops taking customer payments at 6:45 pm Eastern. Then comes the twist about where the money really goes.
