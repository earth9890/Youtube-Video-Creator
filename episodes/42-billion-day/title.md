# Episode 42: titles and thumbnails

## Title options (pick: 1)
1. **What If You Had to Spend $1 Billion in a Day?** (45 chars). The title question is said aloud at ~0:20 and answered at ~84 %. It uses Zenn's "What If…?" shape on an evergreen money daydream, and it's honest: Dave really tries, by the clock.
2. Can You Spend $1 Billion in 24 Hours? (37). The same promise as a challenge. It reads a little more like a stunt video.
3. What If Your Billion Vanished at Midnight? (42). This leads with the rule and the stakes. It's vaguer about what the video does.
4. How Big Is a Billion Dollars, Really? (37). Strong search for the scale chapters (ten tonnes, thirty-one years, twelve cents), but it has no story hook.
5. Why You Can't Spend $1 Billion in a Day (39). True, but it gives away the first half of the answer.
