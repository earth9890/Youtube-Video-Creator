## Checked and NOT used
- Claims that lucid dreaming treats nightmares or phobias: therapeutic claims, left out (no medical advice).
- Brain-stimulation studies that induce lucidity (40 Hz): small, contested; left out.
- Cat brainstem-lesion experiments (animals acting out dreams): scientifically interesting but about harmed animals and contested in interpretation; only the twitching of normal sleeping cats is used.
- "Freud and dream meaning": left out; the script says only that dreams are not fully explained.
- Any "dream text changes" claim: used only as a gag (the cards say goose), not as a finding.

## Unsure / flagged
- The 2021 percentage is 18.4 % of 158 attempts in signal-verified lucid dreams (my first search quote rounded it to 18.7 %; the paper says 18.4 %). The script says "less than one time in five".
- The Barrett/Scientific American figure for the final REM bout (about 20 minutes) differs from the usual "up to an hour"; the script says "can last".
