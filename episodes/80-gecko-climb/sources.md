Dave, Poppy, Mister Pike, the clock tower, the kite, the cat, the town hall and the storm are story devices. The rule card (your hands and feet stick like a gecko's) is the premise. "Forty seconds a hand" and "seventy-five reaches" are the story's. ## Checked and NOT used
- **"A gecko foot holds ten times more than it needs."** Berkeley: "the combined adhesive force of all the tiny hairs lining the gecko's toes is 10 times greater than the maximum force reportedly needed to pull a live gecko off the wall." True; cut for length.
- **Spatula size** ("about ten millionths of an inch across", Berkeley). Not said; the HUD would have been the only home.
- **Seta counts**: sources disagree (Wikipedia "Gecko feet" gives a seta length of 5 mm and about 1,000 spatulae per seta; other sources say hundreds to thousands). Only the Berkeley "two million" figure is used, and the script says "about".
- **Adhesion on rough surfaces** (stone or brick): the story gives the tower glazed brick and a glass face so the rule is not stretched; real gecko adhesion varies strongly with roughness and was not checked here.
- **The Stanford "10 times larger pads" claim** (Science News summary: "Using previous materials, a 70 kg human would require gecko-foot-like pads 10 times larger than a normal human hand"): the Science News article itself did not state this in the fetched text; not used.
