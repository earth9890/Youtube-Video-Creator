## Description
You are not behind. You're just not eighteen any more.

Dave is twenty-nine. On his fridge is a list he wrote at eighteen: by thirty, his own company, a house, and front row at Thunder Wolf. Eleven months left, zero ticks. So whose schedule says he's behind? Suspect one is Silicon Valley, where a 22-year-old said young people are just smarter, yet among 2.7 million US founders, the fastest-growing 1 in 1,000 new firms were started at an average age of 45. Suspect two is the feed: online students who graded the very best essays passed less often than those who graded average ones (45 % vs 68 %). Suspect three wrote the list.
