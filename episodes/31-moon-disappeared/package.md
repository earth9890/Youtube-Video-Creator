## Description
If the Moon vanished tonight, gravity and all, would anything on Earth change apart from the dark?

Dave keeps Earth's night-light on, and tonight he wants one night off: twelve hours of proper dark. So he flips the Moon's switch to GONE and reads the job list taped beside it, one line at a time. A dung beetle loses its moonlit compass and finds a backup galaxy. Coral reefs lose the late moonrise that tells them when to spawn. The tides shrink to the Sun's third, the brakes on Earth's spin come off, and whether the Moon really steadies Earth's tilt is still argued about. Then there is the line at the very bottom of the list.
