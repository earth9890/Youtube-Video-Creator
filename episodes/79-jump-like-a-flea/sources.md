The rule card (your legs jump a hundred of your heights) is the premise. Everything the narrator, Doc or Brain states as fact is below. **Solid** = published source. **Inference** = our arithmetic. ## Checked and NOT used
- **Flea "trochanter" push-off argument**: the true story of the jump mechanism (Sutton and Burrows settled that the legs push through the tibia and tarsus, not the trochanter). Too much detail for a 10-second nugget.
- **Which fleas**: the rule uses "a flea" in general; the dog flea jumps further than the cat flea (30.4 cm vs 19.9 cm mean). Not said.
- **Numericana's caveat**: linear scaling of a flea to human size is misleading; the fair comparison is energy per mass. The MIT notes make the same point (constant height). The script's Doc line carries it.
- **Landing**: a flea "usually twists in the air and almost every time falls on the ground upside down" (Buksh et al.). A good gag, cut for length; Haldane's line covers landings.
- **Long jump world record (8.95 m)**: kept as a scale in this file only; the village record is fictional.
