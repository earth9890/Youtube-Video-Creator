1. **What If You Fell Into a Black Hole?** (35 chars). The title question is spoken at ~0:19 and answered at ~88 %. It uses Zenn's "What If…?" shape, and it's the channel's namesake topic: black-hole titles run x1.48 (Kurzgesagt) and x1.60 (Sciencephile) on the space channels.
2. What Happens If You Fall Into a Black Hole? (43). The most-searched phrasing, but flatter than "What if".

4. What's Inside a Black Hole? (27). Short and curious, but it covers only the last two chapters.
5. The Black Hole You'd Want to Fall Into (38). This is the twist itself (the giant is the gentle one). Honest, but it gives the twist away, so keep it for a Short cut from chapter 5.
