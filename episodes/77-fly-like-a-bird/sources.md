## Checked and NOT used
- **Daedalus 88**: 115.11 km in 3 h 54 min, 34 m wingspan (FAI, MIT News). A strong fact; cut for the 6.5-minute length.
- **Pilot power**: Wikipedia says the Gossamer Albatross needed "on the order of 300 W" in still air; not used (the pedalling comparison carries the point without a second number).
- **The frigatebird sleeping while airborne** (7.4 % of the sleep it gets on land; may stay aloft up to two months): Hedenström 2016 cites it; kept out so the sting stays on one animal.
- **Bird bones "lighter than feathers"** (frigatebird myth): not checked to a primary source; not used.
- The exact weight of the flapping-flight limit is contested (Ellington argues no firm size limit can be predicted); the script says "often taken".
