## Description
2 AM. You promised yourself never again. Then the screen lights up and an hour disappears.
Here's why it isn't about desire — and the 30-minute rule that breaks the cycle.

Most people fight the late-night screen habit with shame and willpower, and both make it worse. This video shows what the habit is really doing for you (escape from stress, loneliness, tiredness), why the shame afterwards feeds the loop, and a simple plan: ride the urge like a wave, charge your phone outside the bedroom, and never miss twice.
You're not broken. You're becoming someone who chooses sleep.
