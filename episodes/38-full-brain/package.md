## Description
Dave has heard that he only uses ten percent of his brain, so tonight he finds a dial on the back of his head, set to ten, and starts turning it. Twenty percent does nothing. Fifty percent does nothing. A hundred percent is the one setting his brain spends all day making sure never happens. So what would really happen if you used all of your brain?

Dave's Brain, a small, tired librarian, explains why there is no idle ninety percent: who stuck the number on in the first place (William James never gave one), why the brain is the most expensive thing you own, what scans find across a whole day, why every neuron firing at once is a seizure rather than a superpower, how the brain's librarians keep it quiet, and what actually does change your brain: practice.
