# Episode 38 "What If You Used 100% of Your Brain?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, the dial, the "10%" sticker, the house with lit windows and the talking Brain are story devices, not science claims. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published measurement or result. **Inference** = our reasoning from a solid fact, kept modest in the script.

## Checked and NOT used
- "Wilder Penfield started the myth" — attribution is weak (Psychology Today piece only); not used.
- "Silent cortex" history — could not source a clean primary quote quickly; not used.
- The 13 spikes/s figure (cortex out-eats the whole body) — true per Lennie, cut for length.
- "We may use only 10 % at rest" (Scientific American) — a loose remark, conflicts with Henley's 24-hour statement; not used.
- Hemispherectomy stories — true and striking, but off the spine and easily misread as support for the myth.
