## Description
Your attention span has dropped from 2½ minutes to 47 seconds — and it isn't your fault.
Three small changes you can make today to train it back.

In 2004 people stayed on one screen for two and a half minutes; today it's forty-seven seconds. Your brain isn't broken — every buzz, red dot and scroll has trained it to chase the next tiny reward, and every switch costs you up to twenty minutes of focus. This video gives you three steps (phone in another room, silence every notification that isn't a person, one task for twenty-five minutes) and one reframe: boredom is where your best ideas have been waiting.
