## Checked and NOT used
- **"Without treatment Messi would have been 4 ft 7"**: widely repeated and attributed to Dr Diego Schwarzstein, but it comes from press retellings, not a primary source. Dropped.
- **"Barcelona paid $900 a month"**: figures vary between sources ($900, 1,000 pesos, 152,000 pesetas for the first round). The script says only "Barcelona paid".
- **Case & Paxson's "1.5 % per inch" number**: real (British data), but the script needs only "a little", and a number would distract from the Persico twist.
- **Persico sample limit**: the "adult height doesn't matter once teen height is controlled for" result is for **white men** (US NLSY and UK NCDS). The script says "found" without generalising further, and says "partly" for the clubs and teams link.
- **Power posing (Carney, Cuddy & Yap 2010)**: the hormone results failed to replicate, so it is not used.
- **Owner bans**: the 1968 "professor looked taller" study and the "1 cm taller in the morning" fact are both excluded.
