## Description
In 1968 the same man was introduced to five classes with a different title each time — and as "professor" he was guessed 5 cm taller.
You can't change your height. This video is about the one thing you can change: how you carry it.

A short whiteboard story for anyone who ever stood at the back of the photo or checked who was taller before sitting down.
We look at why height is judged by status, not centimeters, why Napoleon was never actually short, and seven small habits that change how people see you.
No tricks, no insoles — just presence.
