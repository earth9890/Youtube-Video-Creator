## Checked and NOT used
- Shanidar 4 "flower burial" medicinal-plant pollen: contested (pollen possibly carried by rodents; Kent notes it is "unknown whether these properties were known"); left out. Also the 2020s Shanidar Z work was not needed.
- "Hunted and eaten before bones could heal" (the Mead/Byock explanation): left as told, flagged as unsourced.
- Dettwyler (1991) "Can paleopathology provide evidence for compassion?": the search found only secondary mentions; the skeptical view in the script is sourced to Trinkaus & Villotte's own summary of it instead.
- Liang Tebo "surgical" vs accident: the paper argues surgical; some outside experts have been more cautious in news coverage, not checked in depth, so the script attributes the interpretation ("The bones said...", "The authors write").
- Ötzi, trepanation survival rates, Ebers papyrus: not used (outside the "ancient care" spine, and not sourced).
- Man Bac age: NBC says "adult male who died around age 25"; the script just says "a man".
