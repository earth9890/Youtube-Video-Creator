## Description
James Dyson failed 5,126 times before the one prototype that worked.
This video shows why "high standards" is what keeps you from starting — and the smallest way to begin today.

Perfectionism has risen by about a third since 1989, and most of it is fear wearing a nicer jacket. Failure isn't the opposite of success; it's the data that builds it. So aim for a bad first draft: make it ugly, make it small, make it today — then fix it tomorrow.
