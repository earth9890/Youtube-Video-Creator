# Episode 45 "What If You Could See Everyone's Thoughts?": sources (verified 2026-10-22)

Dave, the thought bubbles, the woolly hat, the neighbour's oven, the sister, the interviewer, Sam, Jo, the date, the train passengers, the lab scientist and the talking Brain are story devices, not science claims. The bubble contents are invented examples of the kinds of thought the studies describe. Everything the narrator states as fact is below. **Solid** = published measurement or result. **Inference** = our reasoning from a solid fact, kept modest in the script.

## Checked and NOT used
- **"35 % for close friends and married couples"** (Psychology Today 2007 summary of Ickes): a magazine figure, not one study's number; the primary friends-vs-strangers paper gives ~30 vs ~20, which we use.
- **Spotlight effect T-shirt study** (Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky 2000, *JPSP* 78(2), 211–222): verified (wearers predicted 46 %, 23 % of observers could name the person on the shirt) but cut for length.
- **Killingsworth & Gilbert 2010** (*Science* 330, 932): verified ("Mind wandering occurred in 46.9% of the samples and in at least 30% of the samples taken during every activity except making love", 2,250 adults, iPhone sampling) but cut for length.
- **Nedergaard & Lupyan 2024** (*Psychological Science*, "Not everybody has an inner voice", anendophasia, 46 low vs 47 high inner-speech adults): verified; not used (no prevalence figure; Heavey & Hurlburt already make the point).
- **"People are often wrong about their own inner experience"** (Hurlburt & Heavey 2018 chapter): only in a "do not quote" penultimate draft and a single case study; not used.
- **Tang et al. counting by sevens**: did not reliably defeat the decoder (4–50 % of time-points), so the script names only "naming animals".
- **"Thought worms"** is press-release language (Queen's); the paper says "worm-like". Not said in the script.
- **"Most white lies are kind"**: wrong; only about a quarter of lies were other-oriented (DePaulo 1996). The script says "one lie in four".
