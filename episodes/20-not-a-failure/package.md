## Description
You are not a failure. You're just early in the count.

Dave got one email that started with "Unfortunately" and decided he is a failure. The famous comfort stories don't prove much, because only winners get interviewed. So what happens to ordinary people who miss by a hair? In 2019, researchers followed nearly 1,200 young scientists who scored just above or just below a grant cut-off. The "no" pushed more than one in ten out for good, but the ones who stayed went on to beat the winners.
