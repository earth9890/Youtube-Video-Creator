## Thumbnail A (floor-raiser, archetype A): "NOBODY?"
- **Image:** big-head Dave cropped on the left (s ≈ 4.3, head ≈ 40 % of frame height), side-eye with a sweat drop, holding up his hand tally counter. Its window reads **38** in red. Filling the right half is one tight wall of identical blank stick-figure heads, all staring straight out and all perfectly still, drawn as a single mass.
- **Background:** flat navy #202C36 with a darker ground band. Three elements in total: Dave, the counter, the crowd.
- **Text:** "NOBODY?" in the top-right band, yellow #FEFE06 with a black outline (haloW 0.09). It asks the hook question without repeating the title, and the "?" keeps it honest. Alternatives: "WHO HELPS?", "38 WATCHED?".
- **Wrongness:** a whole crowd watching and nobody moving, with the scared man counting them.

## Thumbnail B (lottery ticket, archetype B): no text
- **Image:** near-black #05031A. One big white hand, drawn as a thick-outline doodle, points straight out of the screen at the viewer. It sits centred-left and takes ≤ 45 % of the width.
- **One red mark:** a small red ring around the fingertip. Nothing else is on the frame.
- **Why:** it is the episode's payoff ("point at one person") turned on the viewer, with the title "The Bystander Effect" supplying the mystery. A named phenomenon on black with no words is the Calhoun pattern; title words never go in the image.
