Am I boring to talk to? The liking gap: after a chat, people like you more than you think, and your replay is the only one that says otherwise.

Dave talked to Maya at a party for five minutes, then replayed it nine times and decided he was boring. So was he? In 2018, psychologists found that after chatting with a stranger, people rate their partner as more interesting than they think they came across themselves, in the very same conversation. Observers watching the chats could see the liking; the people talking missed it, busy replaying their own mistakes.

Sources:

- Study 4 (UK "How to Talk to Strangers" workshops): partners rated each other 4.3 out of 5 for interesting, but guessed they themselves got 3.6
- Study 1b: outside observers could tell how much people liked each other; the people in the chat could not
- Study 3: the gap was the same in chats lasting 2 to 45 minutes

Try it after your next chat: assume they liked you more than you think, then talk to them again.
