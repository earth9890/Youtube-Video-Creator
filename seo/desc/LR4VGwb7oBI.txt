Why do you forget what you study? Re-reading feels like learning but isn't. Testing yourself (active recall) is one of the best ways to study that has been tested.

Dave read chapter six five times and remembered only the page number. So who stole chapter six? Not his brain. Einstein never failed maths, London taxi drivers grow new grey matter by practising, and students who tested themselves remembered far more a week later than students who re-read.
