What happens if you fall into a black hole? We follow Dave feet first to the event horizon: spaghettification, whether time really stops, and what is inside.

We follow him all the way down, from 640 million kilometres out to the edge: the sky bends into an Einstein ring, the tides turn into a tug-of-war between his feet and his head, his friend on the ship watches his torch fade and redden, and a quiet crossing turns out to be the gentle part. Inside there is no way back, thirteen strange seconds, and a postcard that may still arrive in about 10^87 years.
