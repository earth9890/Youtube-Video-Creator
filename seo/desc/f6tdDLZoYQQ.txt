Do crosswalk buttons actually work? What about the elevator door close button? In 2004, New York admitted that more than 2,500 of its walk buttons did nothing.

Dave's morning has three buttons. One is lying to him: New York switched most walk buttons off by the late 1980s and left them up because removal would cost about a million dollars, while in London a Regent Street crossing took 105 seconds whether you pressed or not. One is working for somebody else: the door-close button can't beat the legal minimum, but in a fire it is the firefighter's button. And one has been working on Dave the whole time: dummy thermostats, Ellen Langer's lottery tickets, and the noise button that almost nobody pressed.
