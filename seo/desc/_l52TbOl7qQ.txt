Why do you procrastinate? It's a feeling problem, not a time problem, and the 2-minute rule is how to stop procrastinating today.

Up to 1 in 5 adults procrastinates chronically. The task makes you feel bored, anxious or not good enough, so your brain grabs the fastest relief it can find: one more video, one more scroll, "I'll do it later." Then later turns into guilt, and guilt into more avoiding. Students who forgave themselves for procrastinating did it less on the next exam — shame doesn't fix it, starting does. So make starting tiny, decide exactly when, and begin.
