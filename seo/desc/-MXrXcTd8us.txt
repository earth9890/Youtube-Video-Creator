Is it too late to learn guitar at 40? Am I too old to start? What a study of nearly fifty thousand people found about when your brain peaks.

Dave is forty. He just bought his first guitar, kept the receipt, and then read that the brain peaks at twenty-five. So did he miss it? Colonel Sanders didn't start at sixty-five, a study of nearly fifty thousand people found no single peak age, and people around sixty who learned to juggle grew new grey matter, until they stopped practising.

Sources:
- AAP FactCheck (2021), "Barely a nugget of truth in Colonel Sanders rags-to-riches meme": Sanders was selling meals from a service station in 1930, at 40
- Hartshorne & Germine (2015), Psychological Science: 48,537 people; different abilities peak at different ages, from about 18 (processing speed) to the late 60s or early 70s (vocabulary)
- Boyke et al. (2008), Journal of Neuroscience: 44 people aged 50 to 67 learned to juggle; grey matter grew in a motion-processing area, then faded after three months without practice
